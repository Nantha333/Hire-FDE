FAQ

Before you hire an FDE.

What does an FDE do day to day?

Works on your problem with your people: sessions with users, writing and reviewing code, connecting systems, demoing progress and tracking the measures you agreed on.

How is this different from hiring a developer?

A developer builds what is specified. An FDE helps decide what should be built, builds it, and stays accountable for whether it works and gets used.

What if the work needs skills the FDE doesn't have?

They bring in Mako specialists across product engineering, data, integration, cloud, QA and AI, and use Mako Matrix™ assets instead of starting from scratch.

On site or remote?

Either, or a mix. Most engagements combine time with your stakeholders and focused remote build work. We agree the model with you up front.

How soon can an FDE start?

It depends on scope and your onboarding and security steps. After the first call we'll give you a realistic start window.

Is our data safe?

Mako is ISO 27001 certified, and your FDE works inside your security policies and access controls.
